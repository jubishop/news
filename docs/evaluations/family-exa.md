### #1 — Northwest Chocolate Festival, Seattle Center (indoor)

**Why it's the top pick this weekend:** It's the weekend's flagship family event, held indoors, and it's right in central Seattle so it's the safest call regardless of October weather. The 18th-annual festival gathers 100+ chocolatiers, and a full day pass includes tastings, stage demos, and hands-on workshops at the "Chocolate Experience Center" — plenty to engage kids. **Kids 7 and under are free.**

- **Dates/Hours:** Sat Oct 3, 9:00am–6:00pm; Sun Oct 4, 9:00am–4:00pm.
- **Location:** Seattle Center — Exhibition Hall & Fisher Pavilion, 305 Harrison St, Seattle.
- **Admission:** Saturday ticket listed at **$60 (with fees ~$69.12)**; weekend and VIP pass options are offered, but specific prices for those tiers weren't published in the sources I read — **unknown**. Advance online tickets (no on-site guarantee). The venue's post is urging a final purchase, but I could not verify whether any tickets remain or are sold out — **not confirmed**.
- **Booking:** Recommended to buy ahead via the festival's ticketing partner; I did not confirm current availability.
- **Duration:** 3–5 hrs comfortably.
- **Travel:** ~8–12 min by car, light rail, or bus from central Seattle — essentially local. **Estimated, verify conditions.**
Sources: [Seattle Center/Center Spotlight](https://centerspotlight.seattle.gov/2026/09/25/the-northwest-chocolate-festival-tickets-on-sale-oct-3-4/) · [nwchocolate.com tickets](https://www.nwchocolate.com/tickets/) · [Seattle Center event page](https://www.seattlecenter.com/events/event-calendar/the-northwest-chocolate-festival-x50292)

### #2 — Kelsey Creek Farm Fair, Bellevue (outdoor, nearby)

**Why worthwhile:** A long-running (44th) local tradition that's genuinely well-suited to elementary-age kids: meeting barnyard animals, horse/pony rides, a pumpkin patch and wagon ride, plus harvest crafts and family entertainment — a distinct experience from a tasting festival and very close by.

- **Date/Hours:** Sat Oct 3, 11:00am–4:00pm.
- **Location:** Kelsey Creek Farm, 410 130th Pl SE, Bellevue.
- **Admission:** **Many activities free**; select experiences are ticketed on-site — exact per-activity prices **unknown** (not listed).
- **Booking:** No advance booking noted; on-site purchases. Expect crowds (a popular draw); arrive early.
- **Duration:** ~2–3 hrs.
- **Travel:** ~25–35 min by car from central Seattle. **Estimated, verify conditions.**
Source: [City of Bellevue](https://bellevuewa.gov/city-news/kelsey-farm-fair-26)

### #3 — 57th Annual Salmon Days, Issaquah (outdoor, two-day)

**Why worthwhile:** It runs the full weekend (Oct 3–4) and is a free, educational draw — **watch returning salmon at the hatchery** — paired with a Saturday parade, live music, vendor booths, and kids' activities. It's a different flavor (river/nature + small-town festival) from the other two.

- **Dates/Hours:** Sat Oct 3 & Sun Oct 4, 10:00am–6:00pm.
- **Location:** Downtown Issaquah (the "Grande Parade" is Sat Oct 3) and the Issaquah Salmon Hatchery, 125 W Sunset Way.
- **Admission:** **Free** (general event).
- **Booking:** Not required. Note Saturday downtown street closures for the parade.
- **Duration:** ~3–4 hrs per day.
- **Travel:** ~50–60 min by car from central Seattle (within the 2-hr outer limit). **Estimated, verify conditions.**
Sources: [Salmon Days official](https://www.salmondays.org/) · [Issaquah city calendar](https://www.issaquahwa.gov/m/calendar/event/detail/14734)
