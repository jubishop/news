## Seattle family outings | October 3–4, 2026

Ranked for elementary-age children starting in central Seattle. Travel estimates are approximate **one-way drive times**; event traffic and closures may add time.

1. **Seattle Greek Festival — Montlake (Saturday or Sunday).** A close-in weekend festival with Greek food, live music and dancing, church tours, and a children’s booth with games and face painting. It runs **Oct. 2–4**; Saturday **10 a.m.–9 p.m.**, Sunday **11 a.m.–6 p.m.** Admission is **free**; food and other purchases cost extra. The organizer says no reservations are required. Allow **2–4 hours**. **Travel estimate:** about **10–20 minutes by car** from central Seattle. The venue notes covered festival space for rain. [Festival overview](https://seattlegreekfestival.com/) · [Hours, admission, and visit details](https://seattlegreekfestival.com/plan-your-visit-2/)

2. **Fishermen’s Fall Festival — Fishermen’s Terminal.** A Seattle waterfront outing with fishing-themed hands-on art, wooden boat building, and a trout pond; the organizer describes it as both family-friendly and educational. It is **Saturday, Oct. 3**. **Hours: unknown** on the event page. Admission is **free**; children’s activities are complementary. Whether advance booking is needed is **unknown**. Allow **1.5–3 hours**. **Travel estimate:** about **15–25 minutes by car** from central Seattle. [Official festival details](https://www.seattlefishermensmemorial.org/events/fishermens-fall-festival/)

3. **Seattle Children’s Museum — Seattle Center (indoor).** A hands-on indoor choice, open **Saturday and Sunday, Oct. 3–4**, under its school-year schedule: **10 a.m.–5 p.m.** Washington resident admission is **$16 per person ages 1–65** (plus a 6% fee); non-resident admission is **$18**, and children under 1 are free. **No reservations required**; tickets can be bought online. Allow **2–3 hours**. **Travel estimate:** about **5–15 minutes by car** from central Seattle. [Hours, pricing, and ticket details](https://seattlechildrensmuseum.org/visit/)

**Why not include more:** Salmon Days and the Fife Harvest Festival also fall that weekend, but their longer travel and the already verified local choices made them lower priorities. The museum calendar showed no special program on Oct. 3–4, so this pick is for regular museum admission rather than a date-specific event.
