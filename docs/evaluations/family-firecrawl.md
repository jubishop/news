# Seattle Family Weekend Guide — October 3–4, 2026

For a family with elementary-school children, starting from central Seattle. Ranked strongest first.

---

## 1. CroatiaFest — Indoor · Seattle Center · **Sunday, Oct. 4, noon–6:00 p.m.**

**What:** Part of Seattle Center's year-round Festál series, a free cultural festival celebrating Croatian heritage with traditional music, dance, food, craft vendors, and family activities. This year's program includes performances by the Seattle Junior Tamburitzans (a youth folk ensemble) and "Kolo Za Sve," an audience-participation Croatian kolo dance. Notably, this year's event adds **Chris' Kids Corner** on the Armory balcony — a dedicated children's area with soccer-themed activities, art projects, and games — plus a "Brač" Batana boat on display with its iconic red-and-white checkered sail.

**Why now:** The 25th anniversary of the Seattle Squid festival falls the previous Saturday (see #3); pairing it with CroatiaFest the next day gives a two-day weekend without repeating a venue. CroatiaFest is the strongest indoor option of the weekend because the Armory Food & Event Hall is a fully enclosed structure, making it a reliable choice if rain threatens on Oct. 4. The Kids Corner is new this year, making it a fresh addition.

**Location & travel:** Armory Food & Event Hall, 305 Harrison St., Seattle — about 2.5 miles west-northwest of Pike Place Market. **≈5–10 min drive from central Seattle** (or a short bus ride on the R-line to the Space Needle station, then a 5-min walk).

**Admission:** Free. No booking required.

**Duration:** Plan for 2–3 hours.

**Sources:** [Seattle Center event page](https://www.seattlecenter.com/events/featured-events/festal/croatiafest) · [Seattle's Child family guide](https://www.seattleschild.com/family-fall-festivals-seattle/)

## 2. Fife Harvest Festival — Outdoor · Dacca Park, Fife · **Saturday, Oct. 3, 10:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.**

**What:** A free, family-friendly fall festival with an unusually dense lineup of kid-focused attractions: bounce houses, carnival games, face painting, a petting zoo, a kids' tractor maze, and over 75 vendors including local artisans and 11+ food trucks (hot dogs, pupusas, dumplings, lemonade, and more). Live music runs from 10 a.m. across multiple stages. The event is sponsored by Emerald Queen Casino and hosted by the City of Fife.

**Why now:** This is the most concentrated "everything in one place" family option of the weekend. The petting zoo and tractor maze are hits for ages 5–10, and the free corn-on-the-cob (while supplies last) gives kids a reason to return between activities. It's a single-event, single-parking-lot experience that avoids a multi-block wandering day.

**Location & travel:** Dacca Park, 2820 54th Ave E, Fife, WA 98424 — about **30 miles south of central Seattle via I-5 S; ≈35–45 min drive** depending on traffic. Free parking is available (shuttle from a remote lot is offered). This is the farther of the three picks but well within the two-hour outer limit.

**Admission:** Free, no booking required. Vendors and food cost extra.

**Duration:** 3–4 hours.

**Sources:** [City of Fife official event page](https://www.fifewa.gov/275/Harvest-Festival) · [Seattle's Child family guide](https://www.seattleschild.com/family-fall-festivals-seattle/)

## 3. 25th Anniversary of the Seattle Squid — Semi-covered · Pike Place Market · **Saturday, Oct. 3, 11:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.**

**What:** A one-time anniversary celebration for the large squid sculpture ("Seattle Squid") suspended over Pike Place Market's Economy Atrium. Visitors can meet artist Patrick Wickline, watch artist demonstrations, and participate in educational activities provided by the **Seattle Aquarium**. This is a unique, once-a-year event — the anniversary does not recur.

**Why now:** The event is free, within roughly a 10-minute walk of Pike Place Market's entrance, and makes a natural Saturday-forenoon start. The Economy Atrium is a covered but open-air space, offering some shelter without being fully enclosed. It's a short, low-commitment outing (4 hours max) that pairs well with a longer outing later in the day.

**Admission:** Free. No booking required. (Pike Place Market is open daily; the anniversary activities are Oct. 3 only. The Seattle Aquarium is a separate, paid attraction but has a small free area at the Market.)

**Location & travel:** Economy Atrium, near 1912 Pike St., Seattle — **effectively in central Seattle; a 5–10 min drive or short walk from a central starting point.**

**Duration:** 1–2 hours for the anniversary activities; add time if browsing the Market.

**Sources:** [Pike Place Market event page](https://www.pikeplacemarket.org/events-calendar/25th-anniversary-of-the-seattle-squid/) · [Pike Place Market Instagram post](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdpqggZJsPd/)
